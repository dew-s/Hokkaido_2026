Sunrise Shin'ei-no-Oka, facing east 05:48 opt-in

The one dawn subject within reach of base: the Daisetsu and Tokachidake ranges take first light across the Biei hills. ~15 min from your Biei base, free, and the gate is open from sunrise.

It also fixes the queue problem. Leave 05:15, shoot until 06:15, then run the ~1 hr 15 across to Asahidake and you are at the ropeway by 07:40 — well ahead of the Sunday crowd the warning below is about.

Costs breakfast and a 05:15 alarm for seven people. This is the same shot as D6, so do it on whichever morning suits the group — not both.

Sunset Shin'ei-no-Oka 新栄の丘 16:43 ★

Five minutes on from Shikisai. The Biei sunset hill — 360° from the crest, west side for the sun, east for Tokachidake. Free, ~30 car spaces. Gates close at sunset, so be parked by 16:15.

Get to Shikisai by 15:30 and look east first: the low western sun puts warm light on the Tokachidake range with the beds in front of you. Then hop over for the sun itself.

Hokusei-no-Oka (~28 min from base) is the shorter alternative if the day has run long.

🗓️ It's a Sunday, in peak foliage season. Asahidake is the single most popular walk in Daisetsuzan — be at the ropeway for the 09:00 car or expect to queue. Going earlier is better if the first run allows.

Sunrise Senbō Pass 千望峠 05:48 ★

The Tokachidake range front-lit across the Kamifurano basin. Then you drive on and reach Blue Pond at 06:50, ahead of the 7:00 gate — two subjects in one morning, and the pond to yourself.

Costs a 05:00 departure — 40 minutes later than the old plan, because Senbō is half an hour from Biei rather than the 1 hr 10 it was from Higashikawa. If it is still too much, skip Senbō and leave at 06:20 for the pond alone.

Why not here Blue Pond & Shirahige can't do sunrise

Blue Pond is gated. It opens at 7:00 — a full 72 minutes after sunrise (05:48). There is no way to be inside at first light, so chasing dawn there is impossible, not merely inconvenient. That is exactly why sunrise goes to Senbō Pass and you roll up to the pond at 06:50, first car through the gate, for mirror-calm water instead.

Shirahige is an evening subject. You view it from a bridge into a shaded north-facing gorge, and it is floodlit from sunset to 21:00 all year. Dawn gives you a dark slot in a cold valley; dusk gives you lit water. Hence 08:30 on the way up, and again on the way down.

Sunset Shirahige Falls 白ひげの滝 16:41 ★

Lit from sunset to 21:00 all year round — your one shot at illuminated water on this trip. Worth doubling back for on the way down.

Blue Pond's own illumination starts Oct 22, three days after you pass through. Just missed it.
